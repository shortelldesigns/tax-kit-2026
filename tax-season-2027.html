What “tax season 2027” means here

You prep records in late 2026 and file the tax year 2026 return in calendar 2027. Mixed W-2 + 1099 earners often also make Form 1040-ES estimated tax payments during 2026 (and the final installment in January 2027) so withholding on the day job does not have to cover all of the side-hustle tax.

  • Estimated tax: pay tax on income that is not subject to withholding (for example, self-employment / gig earnings). See the IRS Estimated taxes overview and About Form 1040-ES.
  • Self-employment tax: Social Security and Medicare on net earnings from self-employment, figured on Schedule SE. See Self-employment tax (Social Security and Medicare taxes) and Topic no. 554.
  • Rates & bases: this site does not invent wage bases, mileage rates, or safe-harbor percentages. Use the linked IRS / SSA pages, or confirm on IRS before you pay.
