2026 IRS Business Mileage Rates: 72.5¢ (H1) Then 76¢ (H2)

Byline: Shortell Designs · Updated September 6, 2026

Split-year 2026 business standard mileage rates

For business miles in tax year 2026, the IRS uses two rates:

Period Business rate Authority
January 1 – June 30, 2026 72.5 cents per mile IRS Notice 2026-10
July 1 – December 31, 2026 76 cents per mile Announcement 2026-11 (see also IRB 2026-29)

Apply the rate based on the date of the trip, not the date you fill out a spreadsheet. Miles driven in March use 72.5¢. Miles driven in August use 76¢. A single annual total multiplied by one rate is the wrong method for 2026.

Charitable, medical, and other non-business rates are separate topics; this article focuses on the business standard mileage rate that most W-2 + 1099 side-hustle logs need.

How to keep a split-year mileage log

  1. Record date, business miles, and business purpose for each trip (and from/to if you use that style of log).
  2. Subtotal miles for Jan 1 – Jun 30 and Jul 1 – Dec 31 separately.
  3. Deduction estimate ≈ (H1 miles × 0.725) + (H2 miles × 0.76).
  4. Re-check the live IRS standard mileage rates page and the notice/announcement text before filing.

If you use a mileage app, export by date range so the June 30 / July 1 split is clear. Do not count the commute to your W-2 day job as business miles for the side hustle.

Official sources to re-check

  • IRS standard mileage rates
  • Notice 2026-10 (H1 2026 rates, including 72.5¢ business)
  • Announcement 2026-11 / IRB 2026-29 (H2 increase to 76¢ business)
  • Publication 463 — Travel, Gift, and Car Expenses
