Filing Prep for 2027: Forms and Records to Gather for Your 2026 Return

By Shortell Designs · Compiled from IRS and SSA public sources · Updated October 2026

Filing for 2026 starts when the IRS opens the 2027 filing season. The IRS hasn’t announced that date yet, so check IRS.gov/filing in January. Use this page to gather everything before then.

Forms to gather

  • Form W-2 from each employer. (IRS: About Form W-2)
  • Form 1099-NEC from each client who paid you as a nonemployee. For 2025 forms, the IRS deadline to furnish them was February 2, 2026. Confirm the date for 2026 forms in the current General Instructions for Certain Information Returns.
  • Form 1099-K from payment apps or marketplaces, if you crossed the $20,000 / 200-transaction threshold. Filers must send your copy by January 31. (IRS) Note: some older IRS instructions still show a lower threshold. Treat the IRS 1099-K page as current, and report all income either way.
  • Your 1040-ES payment records, with the date and amount of each 2026 payment. (IRS Online Account) See 2026 estimated tax dates.

Records to pull from your books

  • Gross receipts by payer. Reconcile them to your 1099s. If a 1099 is wrong, ask the issuer for a corrected form. (IRS)
  • Business expenses with receipts, and your mileage log split at July 1 (2026 rates).
  • Net profit for Schedule C and SE tax: OASDI / SE tax.

Keep or toss?

Keep the 2026 file for at least 3 years after you file, and longer in the cases the IRS lists. (IRS: How long should I keep records?)
