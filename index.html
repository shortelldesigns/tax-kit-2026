What is the 2026 OASDI wage base in this kit?

$184,500, from the SSA contribution and benefit base and Form 1040-ES (2026). Self-employment Social Security tax (12.4%) shares that annual base with your day-job Box 3 Social Security wages. Remaining base (not below zero) is the maximum side-hustle earnings still subject to the 12.4% SE Social Security tax. Medicare (2.9%) has no wage-base cap.

Does it calculate state tax or full QBI phaseout?

No. State income tax is not calculated — you may type an optional state reserve. QBI is a simplified 20% estimate (with a $400 floor if active QBI ≥ $1,000, capped at 20% of taxable income before QBI). Wage / UBIA / specified-service limits on Form 8995-A are not applied; a warning appears if taxable income is above the 2026 Form 8995-A draft thresholds.
